    MSME EXEMPTION — SECTION 1.6.10

    The exemption applies to taxpayers of every kind with annual turnover or revenue below RM3 million.

    It does not apply where the taxpayer has a non-individual shareholder, a holding company, a related company or a joint venture with annual turnover or revenue of at least RM3 million.

    “Related company” takes its meaning from section 2 of the Promotion of Investments Act 1986.
